Step 6 – Final Assessment

Vendor grade: **D**

Ezzi Property Group LLC is a real operating multifamily investor/manager with an active MA LLC and an owner-held Active CSL, but it is not verified as a licensed HVAC/mechanical, plumbing, gas, sheet metal, or electrical contracting company. Captain Contracting LLC exists on SOS (formed 2025) yet has no matching trade/business licenses on MADOL/ePlace/OPSI. For Vixxo trade work—especially HVAC/mechanical—the license posture does not support self-perform contracting.

Confidence grade: **B**

Formation, agent, address, CSL, plumber, and electrician credentials were verified on primary government portals. Gaps remain on official HIC Hub confirmation, NH foreign qualification, independent Google/Yelp review counts, and BBB complaint detail (portal blocking). Those gaps are documented as Not Found rather than assumed clean.

Rationale summary

Commercial multifamily capability exists; subcontracting is acknowledged but not transparent at the license/permit level. License status for the *company* on regulated trades is the decisive negative for SP trade use. Reviews do not show a documented complaint storm, but also do not demonstrate a strong independent contractor reputation.
