# Vixxo Minimum Insurance Requirements — Reference

This is the authoritative requirement list. Read it at the start of every COI review. The verbatim wording below comes from Vixxo's sample/standard COI language and must be matched on the service provider's certificate.

---

## 1. Required coverage lines and limits

Dollar minimums and extra lines come from **Addendum B** (Independent Service Provider Agreement — Insurance Requirements). Classify the provider’s **scope of work first**, then apply `addendum-b-scope-coverages.md`. Treat any blank or "$0" limit field as a deficiency. Limits below the applicable Addendum B minimum are **Deficient**, not “needs clarification.”

### Commercial General Liability (occurrence form, 2013 ISO or equivalent)
- Each Occurrence — **minimum $1,000,000** (upgrade per scope table below)
- Damage to Rented Premises (each occurrence)
- Med Exp (any one person)
- Personal & Adv Injury
- General Aggregate — **minimum $2,000,000** (upgrade per scope table below)
- Products – Comp/Op Aggregate
- Aggregate limit applies per: Policy / Project / Loc — must be checked
- Coverage grant must include Premises/Operations, Products, Completed Operations, Broad Form Property Damage, and Contractual Liability

**Scope upgrades (use the higher row that matches):**

| Scope | Each Occurrence | General Aggregate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default (all SPs) | $1,000,000 | $2,000,000 |
| Vertical Transportation (install, modernize, repair, maintain, or inspect elevators, escalators, lifts, or other VT) | $2,000,000 | $4,000,000 |
| Same VT work at high pedestrian-traffic public premises (airports, shopping centers/malls, transit hubs, similar) | $5,000,000 | $5,000,000 |

### Automobile Liability
- Combined Single Limit (each accident) **minimum $1,000,000**, **or** Bodily Injury (per person) + Bodily Injury (per accident) + Property Damage (per accident) totaling at least $1,000,000 CSL equivalent
- One of the following auto-coverage scope boxes must be checked: Any Auto / All Owned Autos / Scheduled Autos / Hired Autos / Non-Owned Autos. "Any Auto" or the combination of Owned + Hired + Non-Owned is preferred.

### Umbrella / Excess Liability
- Required when underlying GL or Auto limits do not meet the contracted total for that SP’s scope. Occurrence form preferred. Each Occurrence and Aggregate must be populated.
- Umbrella may stack to meet a GL/Auto total. It does **not** replace required Professional or Pollution policies.

### Workers' Compensation and Employers' Liability
- WC Statutory Limits — must be indicated
- Extensions (Addendum B): **Voluntary Compensation** and **All States** — confirm on COI or endorsement; if silent, **Needs clarification**
- E.L. Each Accident — **minimum $500,000**
- E.L. Disease – Each Employee — **minimum $500,000**
- E.L. Disease – Policy Limit — **minimum $500,000**
- "Any Proprietor/Partner/Executive Officer/Member Excluded?" — if Yes, an explanation must appear under Description of Operations.

### Professional Liability (Errors & Omissions) — scope-triggered
- **Applies when** the provider is an architect or engineer (design / PE / RA / stamped work).
- Minimum **$1,000,000** Combined Single Limit.
- If claims-made, record retroactive date.

### Contractor’s Pollution Liability (or equivalent) — scope-triggered
- **Applies when** services include pollution-related operations, including waste removal, transportation, and hauling (also hazmat, abatement, regulated waste).
- Minimum **$3,000,000** per occurrence and **$3,000,000** general aggregate.
- Must include third-party BI, PD, and cleanup/remediation, including loading, unloading, transportation, and disposal.

### Carrier rating
- Each listed insurer should have A.M. Best **A IX** or better. Look up if the COI does not show a rating.

## 2. Additional Insured wording (verbatim — must appear on the COI)

The following exact language must be present in the Description of Operations / Locations / Vehicles section, or in the attached endorsements:

> Vixxo Corporation, its subsidiaries, affiliates, related entities and their officers, officials, employees, volunteers (collectively, "Vixxo"), its customers, the owner, operator and, if required, mortgagee of any site where SP performs Services (each an "Insurance Beneficiary" and collectively, "Insurance Beneficiaries") are included as Additional Insured with respect to the General Liability as per Endorsement CG 20 10 Edition 04/13 (or equivalent), Endorsement CG 20 37, Edition 04/13 (or equivalent) and the Automobile Liability policy as per Endorsement CA 20 48, Edition 10/13 (or equivalent) as required by written agreement. The insurance provided by the General Liability policy shall be primary to any other insurance coverage available. Waiver of Subrogation applies to Additional Insured with respect to the General Liability, Automobile Liability and Workers Compensation/Employers Liability policies as required by written agreement. 30 days' Notice of Cancellation applies.

A generic "Vixxo Corporation is listed as Additional Insured" is **not** sufficient. The full block above (or wording that captures every element of it) is required.

## 3. Required endorsements (form numbers and editions)

| Coverage | Endorsement | Edition | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Liability | CG 20 10 | 04/13 (or equivalent) | Additional Insured – Ongoing Operations |
| General Liability | CG 20 37 | 04/13 (or equivalent) | Additional Insured – Completed Operations |
| Automobile Liability | CA 20 48 | 10/13 (or equivalent) | Additional Insured – Auto |

All three are required. CG 20 10 alone does not satisfy the requirement — CG 20 37 must also be present for completed operations.

For the full list of ISO and carrier-proprietary equivalents (primary/non-contributory, waiver of subrogation, notice of cancellation, umbrella forms, and acceptance notes), see **`endorsement-equivalents.md`** and **`assets/endorsement-equivalents.pdf`**. For state-law constraints on cancellation notices and WC WOS, see **`state-law-quick-reference.md`** and **`state-law-cancellation-wos.md`**.

## 4. Other required provisions

These provisions appear in the Sample COI Description of Operations and must be satisfied on the certificate. Each maps to one or more policy endorsements — see `endorsement-equivalents.md` for form numbers.

- **Primary and non-contributory** — the General Liability policy must be primary to any other insurance available to Vixxo. ISO form: **CG 20 01 04/13** (or equivalent). Addendum B also states that **all** required insurance shall be primary to coverage available to Company or any Insurance Beneficiary — when Professional or Pollution applies, require primary wording on those lines or mark **Needs clarification**.
- **Waiver of Subrogation** — must apply on all three of: General Liability, Automobile Liability, and Workers' Compensation / Employers' Liability, in favor of the Additional Insureds. ISO forms: **CG 24 04** (GL), **CA 04 44** (Auto), **WC 00 03 13** (WC/EL), each or equivalent. Addendum B says all required insurance shall contain a waiver in favor of Company — extend the ask to Professional / Pollution when those lines apply.
- **Notice of Cancellation** — 30 days' notice required. ISO forms: **IL 01 03** or **CG 02 24** (or equivalent). Addendum B also requires 30 days' notice of **material change**; if only cancellation is stated, mark **Needs clarification**.
- **A.M. Best** — licensed carriers rated at least **A IX**.

## 6. Per-requirement checklist (use this when reviewing)

For each item below, mark Compliant / Missing / Deficient / Needs clarification.

1. Insured name matches the SP's contracting legal name
2. Policy dates current (not expired; flag if expiring within 30 days)
3. Scope classified (default / VT $2M–$4M / VT public $5M–$5M; E&O; Pollution) — see `addendum-b-scope-coverages.md`
4. CGL — occurrence form, 2013 ISO or equivalent, all six limit fields populated
5. CGL — Each Occurrence and General Aggregate meet the **scope-applicable** Addendum B minimums
6. CGL — aggregate-applies-per box selected
7. Auto Liability — CSL (or equivalent) at least **$1,000,000** and scope box checked
8. Umbrella/Excess — present if needed to reach required GL/Auto totals
9. WC — statutory box indicated; Voluntary Compensation / All States confirmed or flagged
10. EL — Each Accident at least **$500,000**
11. EL — Disease Each Employee at least **$500,000**
12. EL — Disease Policy Limit at least **$500,000**
13. Proprietor/Partner exclusion question answered (and explained if Yes)
14. Professional Liability $1,000,000 — Compliant / Missing / N/A (not in scope)
15. Pollution Liability $3,000,000 / $3,000,000 with transport/disposal grant — Compliant / Missing / N/A
16. Insurers A.M. Best A IX or better
17. Additional Insured — full Vixxo wording present (verbatim or materially equivalent)
18. Endorsement CG 20 10 04/13 (or equivalent) — referenced on COI
19. Endorsement CG 20 37 04/13 (or equivalent) — referenced on COI
20. Endorsement CA 20 48 10/13 (or equivalent) — referenced on COI
21. Primary and non-contributory wording — present for GL (and extra lines when they apply)
22. Waiver of Subrogation — GL
23. Waiver of Subrogation — Auto
24. Waiver of Subrogation — WC / EL
25. 30 days' Notice of Cancellation — referenced (material-change notice if stated)
26. Certificate Holder — exact name, street, suite, city, state, ZIP
