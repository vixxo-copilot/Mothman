# Addendum B — Limits and Scope-Triggered Coverages

Source: *Service Provider Agreement Insurance Requirements Addendum B + Sample COI* (attached sample still uses the 2026 Description of Operations block).

Use this file **with** `vixxo-requirements.md`. Endorsement form numbers stay in `endorsement-equivalents.md`. This file is the dollar-minimum and extra-line overlay the prior COI skill did not encode.

Company may require additional types or higher limits on a specific Service Request when a Vixxo customer requires it.

## Scope-triggered GL limit upgrades

Start with $1M / $2M. Upgrade when the provider’s **scope of work** (website, licenses, VixxoLink LOS, Case/Lead notes, or operator-stated trade) matches a row below. If both VT rows could apply, use the **higher** limit.

| Trigger | Required CGL |
| --- | --- |
| **Vertical Transportation** work (elevators, escalators, lifts, or other VT equipment — install, modernize, repair, maintain, or inspect) | **$2,000,000** per occurrence and **$4,000,000** general aggregate |
| Same VT services **at or in high pedestrian-traffic, publicly accessible premises** — including airports, shopping centers and malls, transit hubs, and similar facilities | **$5,000,000** per occurrence and **$5,000,000** general aggregate |

If scope is unclear (provider markets “elevator” or “lift” but Vixxo work is only general facilities), mark **Needs clarification** and do not accept the base $1M / $2M as sufficient until the operator confirms the trade.

## Scope-triggered extra policies

These lines are **not** on the Sample COI ACORD skeleton. Require them when scope matches. Absence is **Missing**, not optional.

### Professional Liability (Errors & Omissions)

- **When:** Architects and engineers (design, stamped drawings, PE/RA services, consulting engineering, architecture).
- **Limit:** **$1,000,000** Combined Single Limit (minimum).
- **Form:** Usually claims-made. If claims-made, record retroactive date; flag a retro date after the relationship start as **Needs clarification**.
- **Do not treat as E&O:** routine trade install/repair with no design professional role (HVAC tech, electrician, plumber) unless the operator or customer SOW says design/engineering is in scope.

### Contractor’s Pollution Liability (or equivalent)

- **When:** Pollution-related operations, including without limitation **waste removal, transportation, and hauling**. Also apply for hazmat, environmental cleanup, tank work, asbestos/lead/mold abatement, medical or regulated waste, or similar.
- **Limit:** **$3,000,000** per occurrence and **$3,000,000** general aggregate.
- **Must cover:** third-party bodily injury, property damage, and cleanup/remediation arising from pollution conditions from the vendor’s operations, including **loading, unloading, transportation, and disposal** of waste.
- **Form:** CPL is often claims-made even though Addendum B uses occurrence/aggregate wording. Accept claims-made CPL at the same limits if the coverage grant matches; record retro date. If transportation/disposal is excluded, mark **Deficient**.

## Scope classification shortcuts

Classify from the provider’s stated services **before** scoring the COI. Use the first matching rule; a provider can trigger more than one extra line.

| If the provider’s work includes… | Also require |
| --- | --- |
| Architecture, engineering, PE/RA design, stamped plans | Professional Liability $1M |
| Waste removal, hauling, disposal, environmental / hazmat / abatement | Pollution $3M / $3M |
| Elevators, escalators, lifts, vertical transportation | CGL $2M / $4M (or $5M / $5M if public high-traffic sites) |
| Typical FM trades (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, doors, signage, janitorial, landscaping) with no design or waste scope | Base CGL $1M / $2M + Auto $1M + WC/EL $500k only |

When vetting a company **without** a COI in hand, still record which extra lines **will** be required so onboarding and COI review ask for the right policies.
