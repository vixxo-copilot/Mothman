A {credential} typically authorizes {supervise / contract for building

**self-perform** separately licensed trades such as electrical, plumbing,
HVAC, or sheet metal where those trades have their own boards.

> those trades without the applicable trade and business licenses.

## Vetting rule (all states)

> A valid **general contractor / construction supervisor / building**
> credential does **not** prove authorization for electrical, plumbing, HVAC,
> sheet metal, fire, backflow, pest, or low-voltage specialties. Verify the
> **specific trade credential** (and business/entity license where required)
> at the official agency for the project state (and municipality if applicable).

Aligns with specialty-trade guidance in
`service-provider-vetting-analyst/reference/contractor-licensing-specialty-trades.md`.

## Filling a stub (definition of done)

A stub becomes **Complete** when it has:

1. At least two **official** (.gov) citations for construction-credential scope  
2. At least one official citation separating specialty trades  
3. Lookup URLs for electrical and plumbing (or statement that none statewide)  
4. Reviewed date + suggested account-note language  
5. Linked from this index as Complete (like MA)
