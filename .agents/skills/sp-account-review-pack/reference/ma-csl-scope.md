# Massachusetts — Construction Supervisor License (CSL) Scope

**Status:** Complete (citeable)  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-10  
**Use when:** SP shows a MA CSL but claims or is ranked for licensed trades
(Plumbing, Gas Fitting, Electrical, Sheet Metal, HVAC, etc.).

## Bottom line

A Massachusetts **Construction Supervisor License** authorizes an individual to
**supervise / have charge or control of** certain building construction work
under **780 CMR 110.R5**. It is **not** proof that a company is licensed or
registered to **self-perform** separately licensed trades. Those trades are
governed by other Commonwealth boards and typically require individual master
credentials **plus** a business license with Licensee of Record.

## Primary citations

### 1. CSL = supervise persons engaged in construction

**Mass.gov — CSL Candidate Handbook**  
https://www.mass.gov/doc/csl-candidate-handbook/download  

> A Construction Supervisors License (CSL) allows you to legally **supervise
> persons engaged in** construction, reconstruction, alteration, repair,
> removal or demolition of certain limited types of buildings.

### 2. Regulatory scope — charge or control of construction

**780 CMR 110.R5.1.3** — Board of Building Regulations and Standards  
https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/780-CMR-110-R5-1  
https://www.mass.gov/doc/10th-edition-chapter-110r5-licensing-of-construction-supervisors/download  

CSL testing/licensing covers individuals qualified **to have charge or control
of** construction, reconstruction, alteration, repair, removal or demolition
of certain buildings or structures.

**Mass.gov — When is a CSL required?**  
https://www.mass.gov/info-details/when-is-a-construction-supervisor-license-required  

Frames CSL as oversight of covered construction projects (or RDP control on
larger work).

### 3. Licensed trades are outside the CSL regime

**780 CMR R5 — Construction Supervisors (Mass.gov)**  
https://www.mass.gov/doc/780-cmr-r5-construction-supervisors/download  

A construction supervisor license is **not required** for:

> the practice of any trade licensed by agencies of the commonwealth …
> including, but not limited to **wiring, plumbing, gas fitting**, fire
> protection systems, pipefitting, **HVAC** and refrigeration equipment.

**Implication:** Building-code supervision (CSL) and trade practice (separate
boards) are distinct. A CSL does not substitute for trade or trade-business
licenses.

### 4. What *does* indicate self-perform / contracting authority

| Trade | Requirement | Official link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electrical | Electrical/systems **business** license + Master Licensee of Record | https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-an-electricalsystems-business-license |
| Plumbing / Gas Fitting | Plumbing **business** license + Master as officer/manager | https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-a-plumbing-business-license |
| Multi-board lookup | DOL check-a-license hub | https://www.mass.gov/info-details/division-of-occupational-licensure-check-a-license |

Also see types overview:  
https://www.mass.gov/info-details/learn-about-different-construction-supervisor-licenses

## Suggested language for account notes / email

> Massachusetts Construction Supervisor Licenses authorize an individual to
> supervise / have charge or control of certain building construction work under
> 780 CMR 110.R5. Per 780 CMR R5, the practice of trades licensed by other
> Commonwealth agencies — including wiring, plumbing, and gas fitting — is
> outside the CSL requirement and is governed by those separate licensing
> boards. Accordingly, a CSL is not evidence that the company is licensed or
> registered to self-perform Plumbing, Gas Fitting, Electrical, Sheet Metal, or
> similar licensed trade work.
