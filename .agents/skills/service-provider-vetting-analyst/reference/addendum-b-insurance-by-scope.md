# Addendum B insurance by scope (vetting overlay)

Use this during **service-provider-vetting-analyst** reports. Full COI field review remains `vixxo-coi-review`. This file only answers: given what this company does, which insurance lines and limits will Vixxo require?

Source: *Service Provider Agreement Insurance Requirements Addendum B + Sample COI*.

---

## Always required

- Workers' Compensation (statutory) + Voluntary Compensation + All States
- Employers' Liability **$500,000 / $500,000 / $500,000**
- CGL occurrence, 2013 ISO or equivalent: **$1,000,000** per occurrence / **$2,000,000** aggregate (unless upgraded below)
- Auto **$1,000,000** CSL
- Additional Insured on CGL and Auto (full Sample COI Insurance Beneficiary wording)
- Waiver of subrogation (GL, Auto, WC/EL at minimum)
- Primary (GL at minimum; all required lines per Addendum B)
- 30 days' notice of cancellation (and material change per Addendum B)
- Carriers A.M. Best **A IX** or better
- Certificate holder: Vixxo Corporation, 7580 N. Dobson Road, Suite 201, Scottsdale, AZ 85256

---

## Extra lines and GL upgrades (from scope of work)

Classify from website, licenses, LinkedIn, trade listings, and any Vixxo Case/Lead/LOS notes. A company can trigger more than one row.

| If work includes… | Require on future COI |
| --- | --- |
| Architecture, engineering, PE/RA, stamped design | **Professional Liability (E&O) $1,000,000** CSL |
| Waste removal, transportation, hauling, hazmat, abatement, environmental cleanup, regulated waste | **Contractor’s Pollution Liability $3,000,000 / $3,000,000** covering BI, PD, cleanup, loading/unloading/transport/disposal |
| Elevators, escalators, lifts, other vertical transportation (install, modernize, repair, maintain, inspect) | CGL **$2,000,000** occ / **$4,000,000** agg |
| Same VT work at airports, malls/shopping centers, transit hubs, or similar high pedestrian-traffic public sites | CGL **$5,000,000** occ / **$5,000,000** agg |
| Typical FM trades only (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, doors, signage, janitorial, landscaping) with no design or waste/VT scope | Base package only — do **not** invent E&O or Pollution |

If scope is ambiguous (e.g., “environmental services” that might be janitorial vs waste hauling), state the ambiguity and list **both** the base package and the extra line that would apply if the broader trade is confirmed.
